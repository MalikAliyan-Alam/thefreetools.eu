## Which France-specific utility tools are searched, and where is competition weak?

### Takeaway
France-specific administrative/legal/HR "calcul" queries have very deep autocomplete trees (strong demand), but almost every one already has 8-10 dedicated French calculators, including a fast-growing programmatic calculator network (macalculatriceenligne.com) and SaaS content-marketing tools (PayFit, Qonto, Legalstart, Swapn). The realistic openings are (a) date/deadline calculators with legal nuance where results are mostly articles not tools (délai franc, délai de carence, 45 jours fin de mois, date fin de préavis, délai de rétractation), (b) algorithmic validators that are pure client-side math (clé RIB, clé NIR, clé TVA/SIRET) where competitors are old/ugly but numerous, and (c) the new e-invoicing wave (Factur-X / UBL / CII readers) which is timely but already filling up with privacy-first client-side tools.

## Which generic tools in French have weak French-language competition?

### Takeaway
Generic PDF/image/text tools in French have very high demand but are dominated by iLovePDF, Smallpdf, Adobe, PDF24, Canva and dozens of localized clones; the brand names appear inside autocomplete itself ("fusionner pdf i love pdf", "compresser pdf adobe", "générateur qr code canva"), which signals entrenched competition. The only realistic angles are privacy-first/no-upload/no-limit modifiers ("gratuit illimité", "sans inscription", "sans perte de qualité") and French-specific text utilities (chiffres en lettres, nombre en lettres pour chèque), though even these have many exact-match domains.
