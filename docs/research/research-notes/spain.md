# Spain (Spanish-language) keyword opportunities for free client-side tools

Method note (read first): Researched 2026-09-25. Demand evidence comes mainly from **live Google autocomplete for Spain** (`suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=es&gl=es&q=...`, fetched directly, reachable). A query that shows up as a top suggestion for a *bare prefix* ("calcular", "calculadora", "convertir", "generar", "contador de", "calcular dias") is a strong sign of high volume. Long-tail completions show sub-intents and whether the audience is in Spain or Latin America. **I did not get exact search volumes.** Semrush's free domain-overview pages returned 404 for every Spanish tool domain I tried (calculadoraconversor.com, todaslascalculadoras.com, calcularletradni.es, contadordias.net, iban.es). Only calculator-1.com resolved. The volume labels below (high/medium/low) are my inferences, not measured figures. Competition was checked with WebSearch (a US-based engine, not google.es). Result sets were similar to what Spanish sites rank for, but the exact google.es ranking order is **not verified**.

Autocomplete source URL pattern used for all "AC:" citations below: https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=es&gl=es&q=QUERY

## Which Spain-specific utility tools are searched?

### Takeaway
There is plenty of demand for Spain-specific admin, tax and labour calculators. The better openings are the **unglamorous, rule-based, non-YMYL-heavy** ones: días hábiles with CCAA holidays, the IVA+IRPF invoice calculator and quitar IVA, letra DNI/NIE/CIF validation, IBAN/CCC, rent updates by IPC/IRAV, trienios/antigüedad, lactancia acumulada, test/oposiciones score, and car age from the plate. Salary, IRPF, unemployment (paro), finiquito, indemnización and pregnancy calculators get high demand, but they are YMYL and dominated by banks, InfoJobs, Taxfix, official bodies and parenting brands.

### Inferences
- The best Spain-only bets combine **one algorithm with regional data tables** that competitors keep poorly up to date: días hábiles with CCAA holidays plus the 2026/2027 calendars, rent updates with the embedded INE IPC/IRAV series, and ITP with Hacienda values. Two things make these defensible: yearly maintenance, and a clearer explanation than the thin EMD sites give.
- The IVA/IRPF/quitar IVA/recargo de equivalencia cluster is attractive. Most SERP holders are SaaS lead-gen pages that push sign-ups, so a clean, ad-supported, no-signup calculator that covers all four in one page (plus a separate "quitar IVA" page) can compete on UX.
- Letra DNI, NIE and CIF: demand is high, but so is the number of EMD competitors. Because the intent is simple, a well-built hub (DNI letter + NIE + CIF + NIF-IVA intracomunitario + bulk/Excel formula) is winnable, but it is not a blue ocean.
- Avoid or deprioritise the YMYL labour/tax head terms (sueldo neto, IRPF, paro, finiquito, indemnización, pensión, plusvalía). Brands (InfoJobs, Taxfix, BBVA, CGPJ, SEPE, ayuntamientos) dominate them. Accuracy liability is high and E-E-A-T expectations are strict. They could come later as long-tail companions ("calcular vacaciones no disfrutadas", "trienios entre dos fechas").
- Oposiciones/test scoring ("calcular nota tipo test", "nota policia nacional", "psicotecnicos") appears in autocomplete as a distinct Spanish intent (the civil-service exam culture). The formula is simple (right answers minus a penalty for wrong ones), and I **did not inspect** this SERP, so it is a promising but unverified gap.

### Gaps
- No exact monthly volumes were obtained. Semrush free overview pages 404'd for the Spanish tool domains, and Google Keyword Planner was not accessible. Treat every demand level as inferred from autocomplete prominence.
- The live google.es SERP order was not verified, because WebSearch is US-based. I did not inspect SERPs for: calcular trienios, calcular nota tipo test/oposiciones, recargo de equivalencia, calcular vacaciones.
- I could not confirm AdSense RPMs for Spanish traffic.

## Which generic Spanish-language tools have weak competition?

### Takeaway
Generic file tools (unir/comprimir PDF, HEIC a JPG, quitar fondo) have huge pan-Hispanic demand but are **locked up by global brands** (iLovePDF/iLoveIMG, Smallpdf, Adobe, Convertio). The weaker generic Spanish SERPs are text utilities where the rankers are **translated English tools** or small sites: comparar textos, números a letras (with a currency per country), pasar a mayúsculas, contador de caracteres, calculadora de horas/fechas.

### Inferences
- A pure-client-side promise ("sin subir archivos": files never leave the browser) is a real differentiator against iLoveIMG, Convertio, Apowersoft and others for HEIC→JPG and compressing images/PDFs. Brand lock-in keeps the opportunity score moderate; these are best as supporting pages, not traffic anchors.
- In a multilingual site, "comparar textos" and "números a letras" are the cleanest generic Spanish gaps. Números a letras needs currency presets (euros, pesos MX/CO/AR/CL, soles, bolívares) and a cheque format, which turns one tool into several country-intent landing pages.
- The "sin caducidad" qualifier on QR generators is a messaging hook: a static QR that never expires. Competition is still strong (QR Code Monkey, Canva, Adobe).

### Gaps
- I did not inspect Spanish SERPs for contador de caracteres, pasar a mayúsculas, generador código de barras, or números romanos.

### Inferences
- Pan-Hispanic multipliers: días hábiles (country holiday sets), números a letras (currencies), comparar textos, HEIC, and horas/fechas calculators can serve LatAm traffic. LatAm AdSense RPMs are typically well below Spain's; this is general industry knowledge, not verified here. Spain-only tools (DNI, IVA, IBAN, trienios, ITP, IRAV, lactancia) get Spanish RPMs without LatAm dilution.
- Suggested cluster architecture: one "Fechas y plazos" hub (días hábiles, entre fechas, sumar/restar días, trienios, vacaciones, lactancia) that shares a festivos dataset, and one "Autónomos/IVA" hub (calcular IVA, quitar IVA, factura IVA+IRPF, recargo de equivalencia, números a letras for invoices).

### Gaps
- Scores are relative judgements from autocomplete prominence and a qualitative read of the SERPs. Exact volumes and keyword difficulty (KD) need a paid tool or Keyword Planner to validate before building.
- I could not measure ad load or UX on competitor pages beyond titles and snippets. I did not fetch competitor pages to count ads.
